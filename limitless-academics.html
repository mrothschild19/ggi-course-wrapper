Our Approach

Project-Based Learning

Project-Based Learning (PBL) is a teaching method that inspires students to use their passions and what they've learned on their educational journey to solve real-world problems. PBL encourages the idea that the world needs what each student has to offer. This approach allows students opportunities for real-world experiential learning that gives them the tools they'll need to succeed beyond the classroom.

Limitless students may pursue their education along one of two offered tracks.

Track 01
Traditional Course-Based Learning

Limitless offers a wide variety of core content courses as well as hundreds of electives for middle and high school students. Students follow a structured curriculum aligned to academic standards while choosing electives that match their interests and future goals.

Track 02
Project-Based Learning Track

Students meet required learning standards through our project-based model. PBL begins with student interests, moves through idea development and learning experiences, then into hands-on activities that meet content standards. It develops collaboration, research, community involvement, and real-life skills.
